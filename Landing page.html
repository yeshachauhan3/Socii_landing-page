Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining the SOCII family.

Is Socii free to use?
+
Yes, Socii is completely free to download and use.
How does parental control work?
+
Parents get a dedicated dashboard where they can approve or deny follow requests, manage screen time limits, review content interactions, and switch between parent/child accounts. All notifications for child activities go to the parent in real time, and everything requires explicit parental approval.
Is it safe for kids under 13?
+
Absolutely. Socii is built to be fully COPPA compliant and verified safe for children under 13. We do not collect personal data from minors beyond what's necessary for the service, and all child accounts are automatically set to maximum privacy settings with parental approval required for any changes.
Can I manage multiple children?
+
Yes! A single parent account can manage multiple child profiles. Each child has their own independent set of permissions, screen time limits, and content controls. You can switch between managing each child from a unified parent dashboard with a single tap.
How does the Video Sentiment Analysis work?
+
After your child finishes watching any video, they can how it made them feel — choosing from: Happy, Excited, Bored, Anxious, or Sad. These votes are collected and aggregated into a weekly emotional insight report visible only to parents. If a pattern of negative reactions is detected on specific content types, parents receive an instant notification. The system also subtly deprioritises content that consistently triggers negative sentiment, helping keep your child's feed aligned with their emotional wellbeing.
Can multiple parents/guardians share control?
+
Yes — our Guardian Access feature allows multiple adults to jointly manage a child's account. Each guardian receives the same real-time notifications and has equal ability to approve or restrict activities, keeping everyone on the same page.
